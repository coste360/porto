Boaters book a transit on the marina's **public page**, `https://<host>/b/<slug>`, in a few steps and without creating
an account. Availability is the real one of the system: **no double selling** between desk, online page, API and
channels, because the berth is guaranteed by the database.

<Screenshot name="phone-online-booking" alt="Public booking page on a phone: berth categories with the price for the chosen dates and sizes." />

The public page works only when the instance is reachable from the internet (not with the desktop app alone).

## The boater's path

1. Arrival and departure dates and boat sizes: for each berth category the page says whether there is room and the
   price list price.
2. Preferences, extras and terms.
3. Confirmation: Porto holds a concrete berth for a few minutes (`PORTO_HOLD_TTL`, 10 minutes by default).
4. Customer and boat data, documents (licence, insurance) to be checked by the office.
5. Payment of the deposit or the total: online with Stripe, or by bank transfer (manual driver).
6. Confirmation email with arrival instructions and the **transit QR** for the dock staff.

The transit is confirmed **only** when the payment arrives (Stripe's signed webhook, or a payment recorded at the
office), never when the browser returns. A payment arriving after the hold expired recovers the same berth if it is still
free, otherwise it is refunded in full.

## At the office

*Prenotazioni online* lists the bookings from the public page and the channels, with approvals, documents and payments.
The berth is assigned at booking time; the office can move the boat before arrival with no effect for the customer if
the category does not change.

**Approval**: boats above a length, or configured groups, wait for approval; the payment is an authorisation captured on
approval, with a deadline.

## Settings

In *Prenotazione online*:

- **rules**: minimum and maximum stay, minimum and maximum lead time, arrival hours, closing days;
- **payments**: deposit or total, cancellation policy by lead time (refund or credit);
- **approval** and required fields (documents, insurance, people on board);
- versioned **texts**: terms, privacy, marketing consent;
- **quotas**: how many berths of each group can be sold online per night (dock and API are not limited);
- **embedding**: the domains allowed to show the page on their site, and the look.

## Customer area

With a **magic link** by email the customer sees bookings, documents, receipts and balance, uploads documents, changes
dates (subject to availability) and cancels under the policy, with a preview of the refund.

## Embedding the widget in your website

The page can be linked directly (`https://<host>/b/<slug>`, with `?lang=en` for English) or embedded.

**Script (recommended)**: creates the iframe, resizes it to the content and reports the booking steps.

```html
<div data-porto="example-marina" data-lang="en"></div>
<script src="https://<host>/embed.js" async></script>
```

**Iframe**, where only HTML can be pasted (fixed height):

```html
<iframe src="https://<host>/b/example-marina?embed=1&lang=en"
        style="width:100%;height:720px;border:0" allow="payment" loading="lazy"
        title="Book a berth"></iframe>
```

- Works with WordPress ("Custom HTML" block), Wix, Squarespace, Joomla and any website.
- `embed.js` re-dispatches the `porto:booking` event on the container at every step, useful to track conversions.
  Optional attributes: `data-title` and `data-height`.
- Embedding is allowed only from authorised domains; no third-party cookies are used.
- Payment opens full window; at the end the page shows the outcome with a "back to the site" button.
- The marina's name comes first, with a discreet "powered by Porto".

## External channels

Porto has an interface to connect berth booking portals, but **ships no third-party channel**
([Extensions](/en/sviluppo/estensioni/)).
