The panel also works on a phone and can be **installed** as an app (PWA). Screens adapt to the role.

<Screenshot name="phone-dock-today" alt="Dock staff panel on a phone: today's expected arrivals with the Arrived and Collect buttons." />

## Screens by role

- **Dock** (dock staff): *Oggi* (Today) with expected arrivals and departures, boats in port, away and arrived without a
  booking; *Mappa*; *Scansiona* (Scan) for the transit QR; *Cassa* (Cash); *Altro* (More) with releases and blocks.
  Buttons *Arrivato* (Arrived), *Partito* (Departed), *Incassa* (Collect), photo, note, boat measurement and *Arrivo
  non previsto* (unexpected arrival). A departure with an overdue balance is flagged, never blocked.
- **Management**: dashboard, map and deadlines of the next 30 days.
- **Boatyard**: the day's tasks, hour timer, checklists and photos.
- **Dry storage and lifting equipment**: yard moves and the day's calendar.
- **Rentals**: check-outs and returns with on-screen signature.

The full panel is always reachable from the phone too.

## Pairing a phone

In *Dispositivi* (Devices) create the pairing code or QR and scan it with the phone: the device receives a token bound to
the user and the marina, revocable at any time. With the desktop app, phones connect over the marina Wi-Fi
([Desktop app](/en/gestore/app-desktop/#local-network)).

## Offline queue

When the dock network drops, the panel **queues** the allowed operations: arrivals and departures, photos, meter
readings, worked hours, maintenance outcomes, rental checklists and payments. It sends them when the connection returns,
**without duplicates**: each operation carries an identifier and the server applies it once. The indicator at the top
shows the network state and the queued operations.

Other operations (for example creating a contract) need the network.
