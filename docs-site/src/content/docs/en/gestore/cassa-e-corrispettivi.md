## Cash register

*Cassa* handles the cash session of a desk:

1. **Opening** with the starting float.
2. **Desk sale**: items, optional customer, payment method and receipt.
3. **Movements**: deposits and withdrawals, counted at closing.
4. **Closing**: count the takings by method; the difference from the expected amount is recorded and the **Z summary**
   stays available.

Cash collected by the dock staff, by transits at departure and by rentals goes through the same register.

## Receipts

The courtesy receipt prints on 80 or 58 mm paper or A4 and says **"Non valido ai fini fiscali"** (not a fiscal
document). From the desktop app it can go to a receipt printer connected to the PC
([Desktop app](/en/gestore/app-desktop/#peripherals)).

## Daily takings (corrispettivi)

Porto **is not a fiscal cash register**. *Corrispettivi* shows totals by day or month, VAT rate and method and the
commercial documents. What happens depends on the fiscal module chosen in *Impostazioni addebiti*:

- **none**: the marina issues the commercial document with its own fiscal register;
- **export**: Porto produces the daily summary by VAT rate and payment method for the accountant;
- **desktop app bridge**: a fiscal register connected to the office PC can receive the documents; Porto ships no
  drivers for real register models, which are extensions.

A customer asking for an invoice gets a charge instead, which goes into the "to invoice" export
([Charges and payments](/en/gestore/addebiti-e-incassi/)).
