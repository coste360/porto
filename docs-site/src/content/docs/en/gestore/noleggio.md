## Fleet

*Flotta* holds the rental **units** (linked to boats in the registry, owned by the marina or managed for third parties),
the **skippers** with their hours, the **formulas**, the **extras** and the settings (booking window, day bands, time
between rentals, checklists).

Formulas are hourly, half-day, daily, multi-day or weekly, with or without a skipper, fuel included or charged on use.
Prices come from the pricing engine.

## Planner and bookings

*Planner noleggi* is the units × time grid with views by hours, half days and days: create a booking by dragging.
*Noleggi* lists the bookings and the day view.

A unit or a skipper **can never be booked twice** for the same interval: the database guarantees it. Bookings can stay
as options or be confirmed; states: option → confirmed → out → returned → closed, or cancelled or no-show.

## Check-out and return

From a phone or tablet:

- **Check-out**: ID and licence (data entered by the operator, stored encrypted), contract signed on screen, **deposit**
  (cash, card terminal, authorisation or waiver), checklist, fuel, engine hours and photos.
- **Return**: actual time (lateness is charged according to the formula), fuel, checklist, damages with photos, extras
  and release or retention, full or partial, of the deposit.

A deposit is never revenue until it is retained. The check-out and return report is generated as PDF.

## Quick payment

From the rental card you collect in one step (cash, card or payment link). A customer asking for an invoice gets a charge
that goes into the export.

Rentals cannot be sold from the public page yet: bookings are entered from the panel.
