## Maintenance

*Manutenzioni* has three sections: *Interventi* (tasks), *Piani* (plans) and *Mezzi* (equipment).

- **Plans**: periodic work on berths (moorings, chains, lines), piers, fingers, buoys, pedestals, gangways, equipment and
  land resources, every N months, every N cycles or on a fixed date, with advance notice and a checklist. Porto creates
  the due tasks by itself.
- **Tasks**: from a plan or from a **report** (also from a phone, with photos), with priority, state, outcome, internal
  hours, external costs, photos and documents.
- A task that needs the berth free occupies it for the planned nights; if boats are assigned, Porto first proposes the
  moves.
- An **out-of-service** element cannot be sold and the map shows it. The *Manutenzione* lens shows elements that are
  regular, due, overdue and out of service.

Yard workers and rental staff can file reports; dock staff and yard managers handle tasks.

## Utilities and meters

*Consumi e contatori* manages the meters of pedestals, water and power.

- **Readings** from the panel or the phone, also **offline** (sent when the connection returns), with an optional photo.
- **Consumption** is the difference between two readings, attributed to the active contract on the linked berth and
  **split by nights** when the occupant changes within the period.
- Anomalous readings (negative consumption or above a threshold) are flagged before charging.
- Consumption becomes a **charge once only**, with the metered item of the price list, together with the next instalment
  or at the end of the stay for transits.

The *Utenze* lens of the map shows socket power, water and the month's consumption.
