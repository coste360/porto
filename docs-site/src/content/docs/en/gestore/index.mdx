Porto's **panel** opens at `https://<your-domain>/admin/` (or in the desktop app window). It works on the office
computer, on a tablet and on a phone, in Italian or English, with a light or dark theme. This guide quotes the Italian
labels of the panel in italics, with the English label where it helps.

## Organization and marinas

An **organization** (the operating company) can have several **marinas**, each with its own maps, price lists,
contracts, users and public page. Switch marina from the selector at the top of the sidebar.

## The sidebar

## Roles

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| Owner | everything, including users, roles and provider credentials |
| Management | marina setup, map, price lists, templates and all operations |
| Office | customers, quotes, contracts, transits, payments, dry storage, rentals |
| Accounting | charges, payments, refunds, invoicing export, berth rights and condominium |
| Dock staff | dock: arrivals, departures, moves, readings, cash payments, desk transits |
| Yard manager | boatyard: quotes, work orders, planner, hours; dry storage |
| Yard worker | own hours and yard tasks, maintenance reports |
| Rental staff | rentals, check-outs, returns and payments |
| Read only | map and reports |

A user can have different roles in different marinas. Details in [Team and security](/en/gestore/team-e-sicurezza/).

## Live updates

Changes made at the office, on the dock or from the booking page show up at once on the other screens, without
reloading the page.
