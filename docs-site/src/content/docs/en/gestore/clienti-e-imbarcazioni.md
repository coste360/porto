## Customers

*Clienti* holds individuals and companies with the data needed by the external invoice (tax code, VAT number, e-invoice
recipient code, certified email, address), contacts, category and versioned **consents** (privacy, communications).
The customer card shows boats, contracts, transits, charges, payments, balance and documents.

## Vessels

*Imbarcazioni* records boats with their sizes in centimetres (length overall, beam, draft), type (motor, sail,
catamaran, RIB…), owners and other linked parties. These sizes drive the fit with berths and the prices: a boat does not
go into a berth that is too small without an explicit override.

## Documents and deadlines

- Upload documents (ID, licence, insurance…) with the attached file. Documents and IBANs are **encrypted**; files are
  downloaded only through short-lived signed links.
- Document data are entered by the operator: Porto does not read documents automatically.
- *Scadenze* (Deadlines) lists policies, documents and contracts expiring or expired; deadlines also show in the
  *Scadenze* lens of the map.

## Duplicates and GDPR

- **Merge** two duplicate customers into one, with all their history.
- **Export** a customer's data on request and **anonymise** them when they must no longer be kept.
- Porto collects only the data it needs and never writes emails, phone numbers or documents to the logs. The marina
  remains the data controller.

## Imports

In *Importazioni* (Imports) you import customers and boats, contracts, berth rights and deadlines from CSV: first a
check, with the list of rejected rows, then the application, which can be undone. Berths are imported from the map
([Harbour map](/en/gestore/mappa-del-porto/#import-and-print)).
