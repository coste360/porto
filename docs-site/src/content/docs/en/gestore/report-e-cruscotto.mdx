## Dashboard

*Panoramica* (Overview) is the marina's dashboard: each tile shows the value of the period, the comparison with the same
period last year and the trend.

<Screenshot name="manager-dashboard" alt="Manager dashboard with revenue, charged, collected, receivables, occupancy and allocation efficiency." />

Tiles: revenue, charged and collected, receivables, occupancy and allocation efficiency, revenue per metre of berth and
per transit night, customers and renewals, boats hosted, boatyard and rentals. **Each tile opens the report it is made
of**, with the same filters, and its value equals the sum of those records.

## Reports

| Report | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Occupancy | occupied over available berth-nights, physical and contractual |
| Efficiency and allocation waste | how much of the berth the boat really uses; lost potential revenue and swap suggestions |
| Lost demand | unmet inquiries by reason, period, length band and channel |
| Customers, vessels | customers by category, nationality, seniority, value, renewals; boats by type, length, flag |
| Revenue, accruals and deferrals | the value of contracts and transits split by service days |
| Charges, payments, revenue classes | by period, customer, item, origin, payment and invoicing state, VAT |
| Receivables | open items by age and reminders sent |
| Presence | arrivals, departures, absences of annual contracts, releases and resales |
| Berth rights, cash | holders, fees and splits; cash sessions and daily takings |
| Dry storage, maintenance, utilities | area occupancy, tasks, consumption |
| Boatyard, rentals, sensors | hours and margins; fleet utilisation and deposits; sensor discrepancies |
| Comparison | any indicator between two periods or years, aligned by date or by weekday |

Comparisons go back up to **5 years**. Every report exports as **CSV** (Italian format: `;` separator, decimal comma)
and **Excel**. Which reports you see depends on your role ([Overview](/en/gestore/#roles)).
