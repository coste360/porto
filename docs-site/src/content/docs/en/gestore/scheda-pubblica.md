*Scheda pubblica* is the marina's presentation page, published by your instance at `https://<host>/p/<slug>` next to the
booking page, only when the instance is reachable from the internet.

## Content

- position (coordinates of the entrance), address, contacts, **VHF channel**, office and dock staff hours;
- characteristics: number of berths and transit berths, maximum length, **depths**, seabed type, shelter, access notes;
- **services**: water, power, fuel, Wi-Fi, toilets and showers, laundry, black water pump-out, waste, travel lift, crane,
  slipway, boatyard, dry storage, rentals, parking, restaurants and more;
- **photos** (re-encoded without metadata) and a **simplified map** derived from the harbour map;
- optional: the **transit price matrix** of the price list and **free berths aggregated by category**, never which
  berths or which boats;
- the booking button.

The profile contains no personal data, includes structured data for search engines, stays in your instance and is sent
to no external directory. Link it from your website, social media or the marina's listing on search engines.
