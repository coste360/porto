## Dry storage contracts

A dry storage contract puts a boat on land in a yard or shed for a period (winter, annual, by months), on its own or the
marina's cradle, with linked services (haul-out, launch, washing…). The price comes from the price list (per m², by
band, by month, by area type). The boat occupies the cells of its footprint: two boats cannot be in the same space on the
same day.

## Planner

For each area: the **plan** to scale on the chosen date and the **timeline** with each boat's entry and exit. Moving the
date shows arrivals and exits. Boats are dragged and rotated, also from the keyboard; you work on a draft with *Salva* and
*Ripristina*, with the panel of boats **to place**.

## Exit accessibility

A boat in a full shed can get trapped. Porto checks for every boat whether there is a free path to a wide enough gate
and, if not, which boats block it. A planned exit of a blocked boat is a **warning** in the planner and in the list of
moves.

## Scenarios and optimizer

- A **scenario** is a working copy of an area's placements: you change it freely **without touching the real state**,
  with indicators (surface used, blocked boats, moves needed, boats to place) and a comparison with the real state or
  with another scenario.
- The **optimizer** proposes a layout that places every boat, keeps those leaving first near the gates, reduces moves and
  leaves the largest free surface. It is a proposal, never applied by itself.
- **Applying** a scenario runs all its moves together, rechecked against the current state: a single conflict cancels
  everything. The applied moves form the list of yard moves, available on the phone.

Haul-outs and launches are booked on the [lifting equipment](/en/gestore/cantiere/#lifting-equipment).
