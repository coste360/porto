In many Italian marinas some berths are subject to **multi-year rights of use** (the right bought until the concession
expires, shares of companies or consortia). Porto manages holders, transfers, fees and common charges.

## Rights of use

*Diritti d'uso* records the right on each berth: one or more holders with their share in thousandths (adding up to
1000), type, start and end (by default the concession's expiry), title (deed, number, notary, date) and documents. The
right occupies the berth for its whole period; the holder may moor their own boat or authorised third-party boats. The
*Diritti d'uso* lens of the map shows holders' berths, berths under mandate and unpaid fees.

## Transfers

*Cessioni* transfers a right from the outgoing to the incoming holder, with an effective date, any transfer fees due to
the marina and a check that fees and charges are paid. In one step it ends the old right, creates the new one and moves
future occupancies and instalments. The chain of holders of each berth stays available and printable.

## Management mandates

With a **mandate** (*Mandati*) the holder entrusts the berth to the marina for the periods they do not use it. In those
periods the nights are released and the berth can be sold to transits; revenue from resold nights is attributed to the
holder at the agreed percentage and appears in their statement.

## Management fees

*Oneri di gestione* computes the annual fees of every active right from a table (per berth category, per m² of the berth
or per length band), pro rata for rights starting or ending during the year and split between co-holders. Before issuing,
a **simulation** shows totals, differences from last year and excluded holders. Instalments become charges like those of
contracts.

## Common charges

- **Millesimal tables**: several per marina (general, per pier, shore services); a table is used only when it adds up to
  1000.
- **Annual budget** by item (pier maintenance, water cleaning, security…), each item assigned to a table.
- **Split** per holder **exact to the cent**: the split total always equals the budget.
- Advance instalments, then at year end the **final account** with actual costs and the **balancing** per holder (charge
  or credit). *Rate dei titolari* gathers the holders' instalments of fees and charges.

## Concession

*Concessione* is the record of the marina's state maritime concession, filled in by hand: authority, deed, term and
expiry, fee and its instalments, areas, requirements, documents and obligations with deadlines, which feed the deadline
engine. Porto connects to no external system.
