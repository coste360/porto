## The whole marina in one system

Porto covers the full cycle of a marina and a boatyard. Use only the modules you need: a boatyard without berths in
the water can work with yards, sheds, work orders and lifting equipment alone.

## Why Porto

- **Free for marinas you run yourself**: no fee and no limit on berths, contracts or users. Source available under
  the BSL 1.1, which becomes AGPL-3.0 after 3 years ([licence](/en/sviluppo/licenza/)).
- **No commission on online transits**: payments go through your own Stripe account, or bank transfer and cash.
- **Works without internet**: the desktop app keeps server and data on the office PC.
- **To-scale map**: two boats in the same space on the same night are prevented by the database.
- **Invoices stay in your software**: Porto exports charges "to invoice" and never changes them afterwards.
- **No artificial intelligence, no telemetry**: marina data goes to nobody, Coste360 included.

## Getting started

<Steps>

1. **Choose where to install it**: desktop app on the office PC, on-site server or online server with Docker.
   [Choose how to install](/en/installazione/scegli-come-installare/)

2. **Create the owner and the marina** on first start, or load the demo marina to try everything.
   [First start](/en/installazione/primo-avvio/) · [Demo data](/en/installazione/dati-demo/)

3. **Draw the map** with the generators or import the berths from CSV. [Harbour map](/en/gestore/mappa-del-porto/)

4. **Prepare the price lists** and check prices with the simulator. [Price lists](/en/gestore/listini-e-prezzi/)

5. **Start working**: contracts, transits, payments and, if you want, online booking.
   [Contracts and transits](/en/gestore/contratti-e-transiti/)

</Steps>
